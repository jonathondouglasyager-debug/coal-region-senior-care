## Cross-Referral Opportunities (Death Care + Senior Care)

This is Antigravity's competitive moat:

- **Hospice → Funeral Services:** Families in hospice are actively preparing for end-of-life. Link hospice directory to the death care directory.
- **Funeral Services → Senior Care:** Surviving spouses often need assisted living or home health. Add "Senior Care Resources" to death care directory.
- **Elder Law → Both:** Attorneys serve both Medicaid planning (senior care) and estate/probate (death care). Cross-list in both directories.
- **Home Health → Death Care:** Home health patients in decline eventually need hospice and then funeral services. Natural referral chain.
