**Tone: Respectful, helpful, not salesy.** These are care providers. They chose this work because they care about people. Treat them accordingly.

> "Hi, my name is Jonathon from Antigravity Digital Services. I'm calling because we've built a free senior care directory for Schuylkill County — similar to the death care directory we launched recently at coalregiondirectory.com.
>
> Your facility is already listed for free — I just wanted to let you know it's there, make sure your information is accurate, and answer any questions you might have.
>

## Objection Handlers

### "Our referrals come from hospitals/doctors."
> "That's great — and those referrals will continue. This directory complements your existing referral sources by reaching families who are searching on their own, before they talk to a doctor. Many families start their research online, even in the Coal Region. This puts you in front of them at that early stage."

### "Families just call us directly."
> "They do — but more and more families start with a Google search. When they search 'home health Pottsville' or 'nursing homes Schuylkill County,' our directory is designed to show up. Your free listing is there regardless. The Featured Listing just makes sure you stand out."

### "We're always full / we don't need more residents."
> "That's a great position to be in. The Featured Listing isn't just about filling beds — it's about maintaining your reputation and visibility in the community. When families are comparing options, a professional listing with photos and testimonials builds trust. And if you ever do have openings, you'll already be top of mind."

### "We don't have budget for marketing."
> "$99 a month is less than a single day of care at most facilities. And unlike traditional advertising, this listing works 24/7, 365 days a year. One placement or one new client covers the cost for years."

### "$99/month seems like a lot."
> "I understand. Let me put it in perspective — one assisted living resident represents $48,000 to $72,000 in annual revenue. One home health patient represents $20,000+. If this listing helps even one family find you, it's paid for itself many times over. And there's no contract — you can try it for a month and see."
