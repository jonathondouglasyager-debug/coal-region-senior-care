## Digital Sophistication Assessment

**Overall: Very Low.** The senior care industry in rural PA is 5-10 years behind urban markets in digital presence.

### Website Quality Breakdown

- **No website at all:** ~8 businesses (Kings Home Healthcare, Anthracite Home Health, Independent Living Services, Andsher Personal Care, Heritage Mills, Law Office of Michael J. O'Connor, Holy Family Adult Day, Tamaqua Area Adult Day, American Surgical Supply)
- **Basic/outdated website:** ~15 businesses (single page, no mobile optimization, minimal information)
- **Adequate website:** ~18 businesses (functional but generic, often corporate-chain templates)
- **Strong website:** ~5 businesses (Providence Place, LVHN, Geisinger, Comfort Keepers — all chain/health system sites)

**Key insight:** The independently-owned facilities and smaller agencies have the weakest digital presence. These are our primary targets — they need the most help and have the most to gain.

### Online Review Presence

- Most nursing homes have some Google reviews but don't actively manage them
- Assisted living facilities have minimal review presence
- Home health agencies have almost zero online reviews
- Senior centers and support services have virtually no reviews

## Cross-Referral Opportunity: Death Care Directory

This is Antigravity's competitive moat. The senior care directory and death care directory serve the **same families at different stages**:

- Families searching for hospice are often also planning for funeral services
- Families who find assisted living will eventually need end-of-life care
- Families searching for funeral services often have a surviving spouse who needs assisted living or home health
- Elder law attorneys serve clients planning for both senior care and estate/probate

**Recommendation:** Add a "Related Resources" section linking between directories. Cross-promote in email outreach and on both sites' homepages.

## Key Observations

1. **Home health is the most fragmented market.** 12 agencies competing for referrals — many don't actively market themselves. This subcategory has the most room for premium listings.

2. **Nursing homes are dominated by chains** (Genesis, Saber Healthcare, Edenbrook). These are harder premium targets because marketing decisions are made at corporate level. Focus outreach on locally-owned facilities like Rosewood Rehab.

3. **The LIFE Geisinger PACE program** is a unique offering — comprehensive all-in-one care for eligible seniors. Consider a guide article about PACE programs.

4. **Diakon manages 4 of the 8 senior centers.** One sales conversation could yield multiple listings.

5. **Only 2-3 elder law attorneys** serve Schuylkill County. They are high-value targets because one Medicaid planning client is worth $3,000-$5,000 to them.
